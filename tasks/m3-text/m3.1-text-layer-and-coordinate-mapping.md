# M3.1 Text layer and coordinate mapping

- status: todo
- depends: M2.5
- files: `app/src/pdf/TextLayer.{h,cpp}`, `app/src/pdf/PdfDocument.{h,cpp}`, `tests/test_text_layer.cpp`
- goal: Per-page word boxes in page coordinates, ready for selection and search.
- spec:
  1. `PdfDocument::textBoxes(int page)` wraps `page::text_list()`. Each box has: UTF-8 text, bbox in points, `has_space_after`, and its index in reading order.
  2. Convert `poppler::ustring` to UTF-8 with `to_utf8()`.
  3. `TextLayer` caches boxes per page. Load them lazily on the render worker thread and post them back with the image, since `text_list` is slow on dense pages.
  4. `PageLayout` gains `pageToView(page, rectPts)` and `viewToPage(point) -> (page, pointPts)`, accounting for zoom, HiDPI and page offset.
  5. `TextLayer::boxAt(page, pointPts)` and `TextLayer::textBetween(page, fromIdx, toIdx)`. Join with a space when `has_space_after` is set, and a newline when the next box's baseline is lower by more than half the line height.
