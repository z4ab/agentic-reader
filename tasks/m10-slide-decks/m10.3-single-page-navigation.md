# M10.3 Single Page navigation: keys, wheel and toolbar

- status: done
- depends: M10.2
- files: `app/src/pdf/PdfView.{h,cpp}`, `app/src/pdf/ViewNav.{h,cpp}`, `app/src/MainFrame.{h,cpp}`, `tests/test_pdf_view_nav.cpp`, `tests/acceptance/m10.3.steps`
- goal: Flip slides with the keys, wheel and buttons people expect from a slide viewer.
- spec:
  1. SinglePage keys: Right, PgDn, Space and N go forward. Left, PgUp, Shift+Space and P go back. Home/End go to the first/last page. Ctrl+G works as in Continuous. Up/Down scroll 40px within a page taller than the viewport and flip at its top or bottom edge, and flip directly when the page's height fits. Overflow here is vertical only: a page that is only wider than the viewport pans with the horizontal wheel, a trackpad or the scrollbar.
  2. SinglePage wheel: when the page fits, accumulate `GetWheelRotation()` and flip one page per `GetWheelDelta()`, ignoring wheel events for 150 ms after a wheel flip (key and button flips do not start the lockout). When the page overflows, the wheel pans and one more full notch at the edge flips. Going forward shows the new page from its top, going back from its bottom. Ctrl+wheel still zooms.
  3. Put the flip decision (accumulator, lockout, edge test) in a window-free helper in `ViewNav` so it is unit tested.
  4. The toolbar gains Previous and Next page buttons, active in both modes. In Continuous they call `goToPage(current ± 1)`. In SinglePage they flip like the keys: Previous shows the page from its bottom, Next from its top.
  5. Continuous mode keys and wheel behave as before. The chat input and find bar keep their keys (N, P and Space type text there).
